Executive Summary
Classic NestJS modular monolith done by the book — clean feature modules, DI everywhere, Prisma centralized, and even a circuit-breaker for BNPL/Stripe calls. The concerning bits are the dozens of loose .js/.py utility scripts littering the backend root (check-db.js, reset-password.js, fix-cart-schema.js) which suggest ad-hoc ops, and the prisma.service has 48 fan-in meaning every module is hard-coupled to it. Test coverage is thin (one .spec.ts plus a handful of e2e specs) for a codebase this size.
